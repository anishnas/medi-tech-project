About Us At MEDIXO, we're transforming healthcare through technology. Our mission is to bridge the gap between medical innovation and everyday care, delivering smarter, faster, and more personalized solutions to patients and providers. Founded in 2025, we bring together a passionate team of healthcare professionals, technologists, and innovators dedicated to improving lives. Whether it's through AI-powered diagnostics, wearable health monitors, telemedicine platforms, or data-driven treatment plans, we're reshaping how healthcare is delivered—making it more accessible, efficient, and human-centered. We believe in a future where technology empowers better health outcomes for everyone, everywhere.
